What Bhavani Transport offers

A logistics business that moves goods for clients by road and by water, now with an online booking system.

Roadway transport

Trucks for general goods, containers and time-sensitive loads. Best for door-to-door delivery and short deadlines.

Waterway transport

Inland and coastal vessels for heavy or bulk cargo where cost matters more than speed.

AI-assisted booking

Describe the shipment in your own words or through a form. The system turns it into a structured booking and proposes a schedule.

Booking status

Follow every booking from requested to scheduled, in transit and delivered.

Shipment documents

Cargo photos, invoices and delivery proof are stored with the booking.

Staff in control

The AI only suggests. Operations staff check and confirm the final schedule.

From request to delivery

  1. Customer submits a request

    Cargo type, quantity or weight, pickup and drop locations, timeline and preferred mode (road or water).

  2. AI reads the requirement

    The OpenAI API interprets free text or form input and returns a structured booking.

  3. Schedule is proposed

    The system suggests transport mode, estimated timeline and an available vehicle or vessel.

  4. Staff review

    The operations team edits, confirms or rejects the suggestion in the admin dashboard.

  5. Customer tracks the booking

    Status moves from requested to scheduled, in transit and delivered.

Project scope

In scope

  • Public website with services, service areas and transport modes
  • Customer booking form
  • AI interpretation of requirements (OpenAI API)
  • Automated schedule suggestion
  • Admin dashboard to review, edit, confirm or reject
  • Separate roadway and waterway fleet views with availability
  • Customer and staff sign-in with roles
  • Document and image upload (Cloudinary)
  • Booking status tracking

Out of scope

  • Real-time GPS tracking
  • Online payment for freight charges
  • Route optimisation and live traffic or weather rerouting
  • iOS and Android apps (web only)
  • Rail or air freight

Stakeholders

  • Customers: submit requests easily and get quick answers
  • Operations staff: verify schedules and manage fleets
  • Business owner: professional online presence, less coordination work
  • Project guide and institution: a working, documented project

Why this project is feasible

Technical

Mainstream, well-documented tools: Next.js, Node.js and Express, Prisma, Clerk, Cloudinary and the OpenAI API. No custom machine learning model is needed.

Economic

Free tiers cover hosting, storage and sign-in. The OpenAI API is pay-as-you-go and low cost at demo volume.

Operational

Customers need only a browser. Staff confirm every AI suggestion, so it fits the existing workflow.

Schedule

Each component builds on existing libraries and APIs, so the core features fit a typical project timeline of a few weeks.

Technology stack

About the project

Manual scheduling by phone calls and spreadsheets is slow and hard to scale. This project gives Bhavani Transport a website that captures each requirement, suggests a schedule with AI and keeps a person in charge of the final decision.
