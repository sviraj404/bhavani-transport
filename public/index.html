What Bhavani Transport Offers

A logistics service that helps customers transport goods efficiently through road and water transportation.

Roadway Transport

Trucks for general goods, containers, heavy materials and time-sensitive deliveries. Suitable for door-to-door transportation.

Waterway Transport

Water transportation options for bulk and heavy cargo where suitable routes and schedules are available.

Online Booking

Customers can submit their transportation requirements through our online booking system.

Schedule Suggestions

The system evaluates shipment requirements and suggests suitable transportation options.

Booking Management

Booking requests are stored and reviewed by the operations team before confirmation.

Customer Support

Customers can get assistance with their transportation requirements and booking-related queries.

From Request to Delivery

  1. Customer Submits a Request

    The customer enters cargo type, weight, pickup location, destination and preferred transport mode.

  2. Requirement Analysis

    The system processes shipment information and evaluates suitable transportation options.

  3. Schedule Suggestion

    The system proposes a suitable transport mode and estimated schedule based on the submitted information.

  4. Staff Review

    The operations team reviews the booking and confirms or rejects the suggested schedule through a separate staff administration portal.

  5. Booking Updates

    Customers can receive updates about their booking status as supported by the system.

About the Project

Manual scheduling through phone calls and spreadsheets can be time-consuming and difficult to manage. Bhavani Transport provides an online platform that simplifies transportation booking, processes shipment requirements and allows staff to manage bookings through a separate administration portal.
